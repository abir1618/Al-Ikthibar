About Our Shop

YOUR RELIABLE WORKSHOP IN ABU DHABI Al Ikthibar Repair is a premier automotive workshop located in the heart of Mussafah Industrial Area, Abu Dhabi. With two fully-equipped service centers, we are committed to providing comprehensive car repair and maintenance solutions for all vehicle makes and models. Our team of certified technicians brings years of hands-on experience in electrical systems, ECU diagnostics, airbag repair, and beyond. We combine modern tools with genuine expertise to deliver results you can trust — every single visit. From a simple battery replacement to complex dashboard and navigation repairs, Al Ikthibar Repair is the name UAE drivers rely on for honest pricing, fast turnarounds, and quality that lasts.
